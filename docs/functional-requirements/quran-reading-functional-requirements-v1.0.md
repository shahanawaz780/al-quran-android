# Quran Reading Functional Requirements v1.0

**Status:** Approved
**Date:** 7 September 2026

## FR-QUR-01 — Browse by Surah
Users shall be able to browse and open the Quran by Surah.

## FR-QUR-02 — Browse by Juz
Users shall be able to browse and open the Quran by Juz.

## FR-QUR-03 — Continue Reading
The Home Screen shall display a non-intrusive Continue Reading card when a reading position has been saved. Selecting Resume Reading shall open the exact marked Ayah.

## FR-QUR-04 — Arabic Only Mode
Users shall be able to read Quran in Arabic only.

## FR-QUR-05 — Arabic + English Mode
Users shall be able to display English translation together with Arabic Quran text.

## FR-QUR-06 — Separate English Quran Section
Users shall have access to a dedicated English Quran translation section.

## FR-QUR-07 — Adjustable Arabic Font Size
Users shall be able to increase or decrease Arabic Quran text size.

## FR-QUR-08 — Arabic Alignment
Final Arabic text alignment shall be validated during UX/UI prototyping, prioritizing readability and comfortable Quran reading.

## FR-QUR-09 — Manual Reading Position Marker
Users shall be able to mark their current reading position by either:
- Tapping a marker/bookmark icon beside an Ayah, or
- Long-pressing an Ayah and selecting Mark Reading Position.

## FR-QUR-10 — Marker Guidance
The application shall provide first-time guidance explaining how to use the reading position marker without repeatedly interrupting the user.

## FR-QUR-11 — Local Position Storage
Phase 1 shall support one manually saved reading position, stored locally and offline.

## FR-QUR-12 — Marker Replacement
When a user marks a new position while another exists, the application shall request confirmation before replacing the previous position.

## FR-QUR-13 — Light Mode
The Quran reading experience shall support Light Mode.

## FR-QUR-14 — Dark Mode
The Quran reading experience shall support Dark Mode.

## FR-QUR-15 — Offline Operation
All Quran reading functionality shall operate without internet connectivity after installation.

## UX Principles
Arabic readability, simplicity, authenticity, offline operation and a distraction-free reading experience are mandatory priorities.

## Deferred
Multiple reading bookmarks and automatic popup prompts are not included in Phase 1. Future behaviour improvements may be based on user experience and feedback.
