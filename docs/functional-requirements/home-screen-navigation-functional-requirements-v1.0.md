# Home Screen & Navigation Functional Requirements v1.0

**Status:** Approved
**Date:** 7 September 2026
**Feature:** Home Screen & Navigation
**Phase:** Phase 1
**Operating Mode:** Completely Offline

## Objective
Provide a modern, clean and user-friendly entry point to Al Hidayah, prioritizing Quran reading, continuation of saved activity and simple navigation.

## Functional Requirements
- **FR-HOME-01:** Provide a modern card-based Home Screen.
- **FR-HOME-02:** Display Al Hidayah branding.
- **FR-HOME-03:** Display the product logo.
- **FR-HOME-04:** Display the tagline: Your Quran. Your Daily Companion.
- **FR-HOME-05:** Provide a prominent Read Quran card.
- **FR-HOME-06:** Provide one consolidated Continue section.
- **FR-HOME-07:** Display Resume Reading when a saved reading position exists.
- **FR-HOME-08:** Display Resume Listening when a saved listening position exists.
- **FR-HOME-09:** Show only relevant saved activities and avoid empty cards.
- **FR-HOME-10:** Use Bottom Navigation.
- **FR-HOME-11:** Provide Home navigation.
- **FR-HOME-12:** Provide Quran navigation.
- **FR-HOME-13:** Provide Duas navigation.
- **FR-HOME-14:** Provide Search navigation.
- **FR-HOME-15:** Provide Settings through a top-level settings icon.
- **FR-HOME-16:** Navigation shall work completely offline.

## Navigation
Bottom Navigation shall contain:
- Home
- Quran
- Duas
- Search

## Continue Section
The consolidated Continue section may show:
- Resume Reading: Surah and Ayah based on the saved reading position/marker.
- Resume Listening: Surah and last listening Ayah.

Only activities with relevant saved data shall be displayed.

## Favourite Duas
Favourite Duas shall be accessed inside the Duas section:
- Browse Categories
- My Favourite Duas

Favourite Duas shall not occupy a dedicated Home Screen card in Phase 1.

## UX Principles
- Simple
- Modern
- User friendly
- Arabic readability
- Minimal clutter
- Easy navigation
- Clear brand identity
- Offline-first

## UI Design Note
Detailed visual design, spacing, typography, icons and final card layouts will be finalized during the dedicated UI/UX Design phase.
