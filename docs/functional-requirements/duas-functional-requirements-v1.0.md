# Duas Functional Requirements v1.0

**Status:** Approved
**Date:** 7 September 2026
**Feature:** Duas
**Phase:** Phase 1
**Operating Mode:** Completely Offline

## Objective
Provide a simple, authentic and user-friendly offline experience for users to browse, read, listen to and save Duas.

## Functional Requirements

### FR-DUA-01 — Browse by Category
Users shall be able to browse Duas by category.

### FR-DUA-02 — Category Definition
Final Dua categories shall be defined during authentic Islamic content preparation.

### FR-DUA-03 — Arabic Display
Each Dua shall display Arabic text.

### FR-DUA-04 — English Translation
Each Dua shall display English translation below or alongside the Arabic content.

### FR-DUA-05 — Authentic Reference
Each Dua shall display its approved source/reference where applicable.

### FR-DUA-06 — Dua Audio Control
Each Dua shall provide a speaker icon for starting Arabic audio.

### FR-DUA-07 — Arabic Dua Audio
The application shall provide Arabic audio for each included Dua.

### FR-DUA-08 — Save Favourite
Users shall be able to save a Dua as a favourite.

### FR-DUA-09 — Favourite Duas Section
The application shall provide one dedicated My Favourite Duas section.

### FR-DUA-10 — Favourite Audio
Users shall be able to play the available Arabic audio for a Dua from the favourites section.

### FR-DUA-11 — Remove Favourite
Users shall be able to remove a Dua from favourites.

### FR-DUA-12 — Local Favourite Storage
Favourite selections shall be stored locally on the device.

### FR-DUA-13 — Offline Operation
All included Dua content, audio and favourites functionality shall work offline after installation.

### FR-DUA-14 — No Account
Dua functionality shall not require login or account creation.

## Content Model
Each approved Dua may include:
- Unique identifier
- Category
- Arabic text
- English translation
- Authentic source/reference
- Arabic audio

## Phase 1 UX
Simple category browsing, Arabic plus English display, per-Dua audio and one consolidated favourites section.

## Deferred
User-created favourite folders and advanced organization of favourites are deferred to a future phase.

## Content Governance Dependency
Before content implementation, sources, verification, licensing and usage permissions shall be defined in Islamic Content & Authenticity Strategy v1.0.
