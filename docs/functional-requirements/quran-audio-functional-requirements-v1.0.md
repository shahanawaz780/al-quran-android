# Quran Audio Functional Requirements v1.0

**Status:** Approved
**Date:** 7 September 2026
**Feature:** Quran Audio
**Phase:** Phase 1
**Priority:** Critical
**Operating Mode:** Completely Offline

## Objective
Allow users to listen to the complete Quran in Arabic through a simple, user-friendly offline experience.

## Functional Requirements

### FR-AUD-01 — Complete Quran Audio
The application shall provide complete Quran audio in Arabic.

### FR-AUD-02 — Offline Operation
Quran audio shall work completely offline after installation.

### FR-AUD-03 — One Reciter
Phase 1 shall support one approved/authentic Arabic reciter.

### FR-AUD-04 — Start Complete Surah
Users shall be able to start audio from a Surah-level speaker icon.

### FR-AUD-05 — Start from Selected Ayah
Users shall be able to start audio from a selected Ayah.

### FR-AUD-06 — Continue Subsequent Ayahs
Audio started from a selected Ayah shall continue with subsequent Ayahs.

### FR-AUD-07 — Listen Without Reading
Users shall be able to listen without actively reading Quran text.

### FR-AUD-08 — Current Ayah Indication
When the reading screen is active during audio playback, the currently playing Ayah shall be visually indicated.

### FR-AUD-09 — Audio State Indicator
The audio icon/control shall visually change based on whether audio is playing or stopped.

### FR-AUD-10 — Stop Audio
Users shall be able to manually stop Quran audio.

### FR-AUD-11 — Stop on Screen Lock
Quran audio shall automatically stop when the mobile screen is locked.

### FR-AUD-12 — Stop on Minimize
Quran audio shall automatically stop when the application is minimized.

### FR-AUD-13 — Stop on App Switch
Quran audio shall automatically stop when the user switches away from the application.

### FR-AUD-14 — Save Listening Position
The application shall save the last audio listening position locally.

### FR-AUD-15 — Resume or Start Again
When the user returns, the application shall offer Resume Listening or Start Again.

### FR-AUD-16 — No Account Required
Audio listening position shall work without login or account.

### FR-AUD-17 — No English Audio
English Quran translation audio shall not be included in Phase 1.

## Saved Audio Data
The application shall locally retain the relevant Surah and current/last listening Ayah.

## UX Principles
Simplicity, offline operation, easy start/stop, authentic recitation, user control, minimal controls and no login are mandatory.

## Out of Scope
- Multiple reciters
- English translation audio
- Background audio playback
- Audio while screen is locked
- Advanced audio player controls
- Custom volume controls
- Login/account-based synchronization
- Cloud audio streaming
