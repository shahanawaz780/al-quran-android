# Business Requirements v1.0

**Status:** Approved  
**Date:** 7 September 2026

## Core Needs
1. Read Quran
2. Listen to Quran
3. Read Duas
4. Listen to Duas
5. Search Quran & Duas

## Mandatory Requirements
- Complete offline operation immediately after installation
- Arabic Quran and Dua content
- English Quran and Dua translations
- Arabic Quran and Dua audio included offline
- English UI
- Offline search
- AI-assisted discovery direction
- Authentic and validated content
- No advertisements
- High Arabic readability and user-friendly UI/UX

## Non-Negotiable Principles
Arabic readability; complete offline operation; simplicity; authenticity; no advertisements; complete Arabic audio content.
