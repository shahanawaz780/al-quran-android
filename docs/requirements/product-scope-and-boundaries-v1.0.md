# Product Scope & Boundaries Workshop v1.0

**Status:** In Progress

## Purpose
Define what belongs in the initial Al Hidayah product and what is explicitly deferred.

## Current confirmed core direction
- Read Quran in Arabic
- Read English Quran translation in a separate section
- Listen to complete Quran in Arabic offline
- Read Duas in Arabic with English translation
- Listen to Duas in Arabic offline
- Search Quran and Duas offline
- English application UI
- Offline-first operation
- No advertisements
- Simple and highly readable Arabic-focused UX

Detailed MVP scope is pending Business Owner answers and approval.
