# Project Vision & Business Requirements v1.0

**Status:** Approved  
**Date:** 7 September 2026

## Project Vision

Build a simple, user-friendly, authentic and trustworthy digital Quran application that enables people worldwide to conveniently read, listen to and engage with the Quran and Duas as part of daily life.

The application will be offline-first, modern, AI-assisted and globally accessible.

## Target Users

Any user worldwide without restrictions based on age, gender, religion, nationality, demographic background or geographic location.

## Geographic Market

Global.

## Business Objectives

1. Provide a free Islamic service and community benefit.
2. Build a sustainable long-term digital Islamic product.

## Product Identity

- Simple
- User-friendly
- Authentic
- Trustworthy
- Modern
- AI-assisted
- Offline-first
- Educational
- Family-friendly

## Core Product Principles

1. Accessibility
2. Offline-First
3. Simplicity
4. Authenticity & Trust
5. User-Friendly Design
6. AI-Assisted
7. Global Accessibility
8. Long-Term Sustainability

## UX Priority — Arabic Quran Text

Arabic Quran text readability and overall UI/UX are top product priorities.

Future UX/UI and technical decisions must prioritize:

- Clear and highly readable Arabic Quran text
- Appropriate Arabic typography and rendering
- Comfortable reading size and line spacing
- Clean, distraction-free reading experience
- User-controlled readability settings where appropriate
- Validation of Arabic text display across Android devices

Detailed Arabic typography, Quran text source, rendering and UI/UX standards will be defined in a dedicated UX/UI and content architecture checkpoint before implementation.
